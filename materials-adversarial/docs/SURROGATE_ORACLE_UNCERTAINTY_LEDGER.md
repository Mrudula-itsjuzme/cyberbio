# Surrogate Oracle Uncertainty Ledger

This document categorizes all sources of uncertainty in the CALIBRATABLE_SURROGATE protocol.

## 1. UNKNOWN (Cannot be resolved without original authors)
- **Unknown original DFT functional**: We do not know what exact functional (e.g., PBE, B3LYP) was used to generate the dataset.
- **Unknown original structure**: The exact 3D geometries (e.g., unit cell dimensions, atomic positions) are missing from the dataset.

## 2. UNRESOLVED (Could potentially be resolved with extensive effort but currently unaccounted for)
- **PBE approximation**: Assuming PBE is used, its inherent bandgap underestimation compared to hybrid functionals or GW is uncorrected.
- **Smearing**: The choice of smearing width and type for semiconductors/insulators may affect edge states slightly.

## 3. MEASURABLE (Can be quantified by varying the parameter)
- **Oligomer-length dependence**: The bandgap converges as $n \to \infty$. We measure $n=2, 3, 4$ to quantify this.
- **Conformer choice**: The variation in electronic gap across different conformers generated by the force-field.
- **Cutoff dependence**: Sensitivity of the gap to `ecutwfc` and `ecutrho`.
- **Supercell/vacuum dependence**: Interaction between periodic images due to insufficient vacuum padding.
- **Pseudopotential dependence**: Differences introduced by SSSP efficiency vs. precision or other families.

## 4. CONTROLLED (Fixed by protocol design)
- **Capping**: We consistently use Hydrogen atoms to cap the finite oligomers.
- **Preoptimization**: We use MMFF94 with a UFF fallback for all structures.
- **Finite-vs-periodic representation**: We explicitly treat the system as a finite molecule in a large supercell (Gamma point only) rather than a 1D periodic chain with a k-point mesh.

## 5. KNOWN
- **Oligomer truncation**: We know that $n=2,3,4$ are shorter than the true infinite chain.
